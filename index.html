I work on who benefits from natural resources, and whether the public can see it.

A decade across the Asian Development Bank, the Natural Resource Governance Institute, Oxfam, ECORYS, and the Philippine Department of Finance, in more than thirty countries. I take consulting work independently, alongside my data practice at Per Capita Insights.

What I take on

Extractives governance
Licensing regimes, beneficial ownership, contract disclosure, and revenue transparency — from diagnostic through to published data.
Data infrastructure
Schemas, data dictionaries, validation pipelines, and the unglamorous work that makes a public dataset usable more than once.
Public financial management
Revenue analysis, trade-gap and misinvoicing work, and fiscal diagnostics for reform programmes.
Applied research
Political demography, spatial economics, and electoral analysis, mostly on the Philippines.
